📖 Music Theory Basics

🎼 The Staff

A staff consists of 5 horizontal lines and 4 spaces. Music notes are placed on these lines and spaces to show their pitch. Each position represents a different musical note.

🎹 Clefs

Treble Clef (𝄞): Used for high notes (violin, flute, trumpet)

Bass Clef (𝄢): Used for low notes (cello, bass, trombone)

⏱️ Time Signatures

4/4 (Common Time): 4 beats per measure. Most popular in modern music.

3/4 (Waltz): 3 beats per measure. Often used in classical pieces.

🎵 Notes & Duration

Whole Note (𝅝): 4 beats

Half Note (𝅗𝅥): 2 beats

Quarter Note (𝅘𝅥): 1 beat

📚 Quick Start Guide

Follow these step-by-step instructions to create your first composition with TuneCraft.

🚀 Getting Started

Step 1: Access the Editor

Click on "Music Editor" from the sidebar to access the composition editor. You'll see a blank staff ready for your symbols.

Step 2: Select Clef

Choose a clef symbol (Treble 𝄞 or Bass 𝄢) from the left panel. This determines the pitch range of your composition.

Step 3: Add Time Signature

Select a time signature (4/4 is most common) and place it right after your clef on the staff.

Step 4: Place Notes

Choose note symbols and click on the staff to place them. Different notes have different durations (whole, half, quarter, eighth).

Step 5: Play & Listen

Click the Play button to hear your composition. Use the tempo slider to adjust the speed to your preference.

Step 6: Save Your Work

Click the Save button to store your composition in your dashboard. You can access it anytime to edit or share.

🎯 Important Tips

  • Always start with a clef - It must be the first symbol on your staff
  • Place time signature right after clef - This tells how many beats per measure
  • Drag to move symbols - Click and hold any symbol to reposition it
  • Hover to delete - Hover over a symbol to reveal the delete (×) button
  • Use zoom for precision - Zoom in to place notes accurately
  • Save frequently - Don't lose your work, save every few minutes

💡 Pro Tips & Best Practices

Master these techniques to become a TuneCraft expert and create professional-quality compositions.

Advanced Techniques

⚡ Efficient Placement

Use the grid to align symbols. When you zoom to 150%, symbols snap more precisely to staff positions. This is ideal for detailed work.

🎵 Multiple Staffs

Create multi-instrument pieces by clicking "+ Add Sheet". Organize them logically: melody on top, accompaniment below, just like traditional scores.

🔍 Zooming Strategies

Start at 100% zoom for overview, zoom to 150% for precise note placement, zoom out to 50% to see your entire composition layout.

🎼 Professional Layout

Follow standard notation: Clef → Time Signature → Notes. Leave proper spacing between notes for readability. Use rests to maintain rhythm.

🎶 Tempo Control

Adjust tempo using the slider in the control panel. Slower tempos (60-80 BPM) are ideal for learning. Faster tempos (120+ BPM) for energetic pieces.

💾 Auto-Save Habit

Build a habit of saving every 5-10 minutes. Your compositions are stored in the database and can be accessed from the Dashboard anytime.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Forgetting the clef - Always place a clef first, or notes won't be readable
  • Wrong clef for instrument - Use Treble (𝄞) for high notes, Bass (𝄢) for low notes
  • Not saving frequently - Save your work regularly to avoid data loss
  • Overcrowding the staff - Space your notes properly for professional appearance
  • Ignoring time signatures - Ensure notes fit within measure constraints
  • Not using zoom properly - Precision matters in music notation
