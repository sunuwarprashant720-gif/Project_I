🎵 Music Theory

Learn the basics of music notation, clefs, and timing

Music Theory Basics

Music theory helps you understand how music works. It covers everything from reading sheet music to creating melodies.

🎼

Staff & Notation

The staff has five lines and four spaces where notes are written to show pitch.

🎵

Notes & Rests

Notes represent sounds, rests represent silence. Different shapes mean different lengths.

🎹

Clefs

Clefs tell you which notes correspond to which lines on the staff.

⏱️

Rhythm

Timing and rhythm patterns are organized using time signatures.

Understanding Clefs

Clefs are symbols at the beginning of a staff that show which notes go on which lines.

𝄞

Treble Clef

Treble Clef (G Clef)

For high-pitched instruments

The curl circles around the G line. Used for violin, flute, trumpet, and piano right hand.

𝄢

Bass Clef

Bass Clef (F Clef)

For low-pitched instruments

The two dots surround the F line. Used for cello, bassoon, trombone, and piano left hand.

Notes & Timing
